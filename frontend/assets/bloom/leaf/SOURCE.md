# Leaf asset provenance

- Reference: Ann Nguyen, Bloom QR, https://www.bubbbly.com/bloom
- Public asset: https://www.bubbbly.com/models/qr-leaves.glb
- Geometry and base-color texture extracted on 2026-09-22 for this local prototype.
- `geometry.json` preserves the mesh positions, normals, UV coordinates and indices.
- `color.jpg` is the embedded base-color texture, without repainting.
